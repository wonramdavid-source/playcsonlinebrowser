## 🇨🇳 中文说明

### 📖 项目简介

**铁潮 (IRON TIDE)** 是一款基于 WebGL 技术开发的免费在线 CS 风格网页第一人称射击（FPS）游戏。无需下载安装客户端或注册账号，使用电脑浏览器搭配键盘和鼠标即可随时随地体验硬核、爽快的射击对战。

> **免责声明**：本项目为独立开发的 CS 风格网页版游戏，并非 Valve 官方产品，亦非 CS 1.6 的完整移植。部分经典地图使用了 CS 1.6 相关素材，实际武器弹道、规则机制以本游戏内体验为准。

### ✨ 核心特色

- **免安装 / 免注册**：打开网页秒进大厅，本地缓存与轻量加载。
- **多人联机**：支持一键快速匹配在线对局，或自建房间分享专属链接/房间码邀请好友组队。
- **单人 AI 训练**：支持离线级体验的单机模式，自由调节机器人（Bot）数量与难度，练习压枪（AK-47 / M4A1-S）与架狙（AWP）。
- **多种特色模式**：
  - **经典爆破**：装包、拆包、战术攻防。
  - **快节奏交火 / 死斗**：经典复刻地图的短兵相接。
  - **人质解救**：经典反恐救援玩法。
  - **丧尸模式（合作防守）**：支持组队合作抵挡 30 波尸潮进攻。
  - **丧尸模式（经典感染）**：开局随机产生母体丧尸，展开人类与丧尸阵营的感染攻防。

## 🇬🇧 English Documentation

### 📖 Overview

**IRON TIDE (铁潮)** is a free-to-play, browser-based tactical first-person shooter (FPS) inspired by classic Counter-Strike, built with WebGL. No client download, installation, or account registration required. Jump straight into the battle using your desktop browser with keyboard and mouse controls.

> **Disclaimer**: This is an independently developed CS-style web game. It is not an official Valve product, nor is it a complete 1:1 port of CS 1.6. Some classic maps utilize CS 1.6 assets, while weapon mechanics, rules, and controls are subject to this game's specific implementation.

### ✨ Features

- **Instant Access**: Zero installation, no sign-up required. Launch straight into the lobby via your browser.
- **Multiplayer Matchmaking**: Quick-match online, or host custom rooms and share invite links/room codes with friends.
- **Single-Player Practice**: Play offline against customizable AI bots with adjustable difficulty. Practice recoil control (AK-47 / M4A1-S) and sniper positioning (AWP).
- **Multiple Game Modes**:
  - **Bomb Defusal**: Classic tactical plant-and-defuse objectives.
  - **Fast-Paced Arena / Deathmatch**: Close-quarters combat on compact maps.
  - **Hostage Rescue**: Infiltrate and extract hostages to safety.
  - **Zombie Horde (Co-op Defense)**: Team up to survive against 30 waves of undead.
  - **Zombie Classic (Infection)**: Random host selection at round start for humans vs. zombies infection gameplay.

### ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I play on mobile or tablet?</b></summary>
The game is built around WebGL2 rendering and dedicated keyboard/mouse input. It is intended for desktop environments and is not optimized for mobile devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I play completely solo?</b></summary>
Yes. Select "Single Player" in the lobby to set bot counts and difficulty levels. For Classic Zombie mode, ensure bots are enabled when playing alone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where is game progress saved?</b></summary>
Player levels, badges, and local statistics are stored directly in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data or switching devices will not automatically sync your data.
</details>
